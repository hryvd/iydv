Google Sheets Backend API

Connect your deployed Apps Script Web App URL to sync all athlete workouts, weights, goals, and skills directly into IYDV_OneUp_Member_Database.xlsx!

How to Setup:
1. Open your Google Drive and upload IYDV_OneUp_Member_Database.xlsx.
2. Open it in Google Sheets → Extensions → Apps Script.
3. Copy the Apps Script code from the Excel "Apps Script (API)" tab.
4. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Access: Anyone).
5. Paste the deployment URL above and click Save.
Data Privacy Notice

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) of the Philippines, the BatState-U Indak Yaman Dance Varsity collects physical stats (weight, height, BMI) and athletic logs solely for varsity strength & conditioning tracking and coach evaluations. Your records are protected and stored in accordance with university athletic data privacy guidelines.
